# Business model assumptions

## Current hypothesis
B2B SaaS subscriptions for cleaning and security operators in Germany, initially Berlin. One platform supports cleaning-only, security-only, and combined organizations.

## Pricing
No prices are validated. Earlier price points are hypotheses only and must not be implemented as billing rules. Validate customer segments, willingness to pay, employee/site limits, and support needs through discovery before billing integration.

## Commercialization sequence
1. Validate operational pain with prospective operators.
2. Pilot core scheduling, time recording, and service evidence with fictional/test data or an approved controlled pilot.
3. Define packages and usage limits from observed value.
4. Select billing provider and review tax/invoicing obligations.
5. Implement subscription lifecycle and cancellation/data export.

Avoid paid infrastructure until explicitly approved.
