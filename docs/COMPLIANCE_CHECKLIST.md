# Compliance checklist (verification required)

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice or a claim of compliance. Obtain qualified German/EU legal and privacy review before commercial launch.

- [ ] Define controller/processor roles, purposes, legal bases, and data inventory.
- [ ] Review GDPR/DSGVO transparency, data subject rights, processor agreements, subprocessors, and international transfers.
- [ ] Minimize employee profile, location, attendance, photo, and incident data.
- [ ] Define retention/deletion schedules and legal holds.
- [ ] Review employee monitoring, access logging, and works council implications where applicable.
- [ ] Validate working-time record requirements and correction/audit trails with counsel.
- [ ] Review §34a GewO, qualification documentation, and operator obligations with qualified professionals; software does not grant authorization.
- [ ] Validate German invoicing and applicable e-invoicing obligations before adding billing.
- [ ] Select hosting/database regions and execute appropriate data processing agreements.
- [ ] Review security controls, breach response, backups, and restore evidence.
- [ ] Establish privacy-safe preview fixtures and prohibit production personal data in previews.
- [ ] Prepare accessible privacy notices and deletion/export workflows.
